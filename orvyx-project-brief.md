# Orvyx — LifePod 72 Website Brief

## Client & Arrangement
- Lead came through Dankata — a friend of his needs a website for their product, Orvyx / LifePod 72.
- Budget: ~€300–400 total, most of which goes to Miro.
- Working principle: no work begins until the client has some skin in the game upfront (deposit/partial payment).

## The Product
LifePod 72 is a hard case system designed to help someone survive 72 hours in an emergency. It's built as modular kits inside one case:

- **Purification & bio-defense**: Sawyer microfiltration (mechanical), Katadyn Micropur Forte tablets (chemical), titanium cup (thermal/container)
- **Mech-Tac module**: Morakniv field knife, Gerber multitool (Black Oxide)
- **Comfort & Lounge module**: matte black stainless flask, Hoyo de Monterrey cigar in aluminum tube
- **Ignition module**: triple-jet torch lighter, BCB stormproof matches, ferrocerium rod
- **Case shell**: impact-resistant polymer, IP67 waterproof, auto pressure-equalizing valve
- **Light module**: Fenix E28R V2.0 flashlight, Nitecore HC68 headlamp, solar/dynamo emergency radio
- **Power module**: 30,000mAh power bank with digital display
- **Hydration module**: Sawyer Squeeze, low-profile reservoir, maintenance/backwash kit
- Case dimensions: 45cm x 37cm x 18cm

Brand: dark, minimal, engineering-driven tone — Bulgarian copy leans on words like "autonomy," "engineering," "system complete." Logo/wordmark: ORVYX.

Existing assets: real product photos (case + contents laid out on dark surface), Facebook page with ~19 followers, no site yet.

## Design Direction (decided so far)
- Inspiration: scroll-driven, cinematic sites like Alpenglow — hero video/imagery, text reveals on scroll, case "opening" and contents laying out as you scroll.
- **Decision: build the scroll/animation layer in code first, using real product photos — not Higgsfield or AI-generated video.** Structure, scroll logic, copy, and layout are the reusable core of the work regardless of visuals.
- Higgsfield (AI video/image generator, ~$15–99/mo tiers) is a fallback only — if a specific section falls flat with real photos, we'd regenerate just that section's visual, not rebuild the site. Not budgeted for by default.
